Tokuma — the circular economy ERP

Run your business. Prove your impact. Unlock capital. Tokuma turns everyday operations — sales, materials and suppliers — into a verified circularity score that lenders and investors trust.

For businesses

  • Live circularity rate calculated from sales and each product's bill of materials
  • Inventory tracking with automatic reorder points from supplier lead times
  • Supplier reliability scores from lead time, on-time delivery and certifications
  • AI circular insights, funding eligibility and estimated APR

For investors

  • Verified impact data and funding readiness scores
  • Supply-chain risk visibility and one-click reports
